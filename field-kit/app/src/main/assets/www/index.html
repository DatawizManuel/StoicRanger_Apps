Which pattern, when

Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy or tingly. Never practise breath holds in or near water, or while driving. This is training guidance, not medical advice.

Shooting helpers

Every result is an estimate to start from; confirm it on the range and keep your own data.

Come-ups

Highlighted: zero distance. Bronze rows: bullet below 1.2× speed of sound, less predictable.

Same solver as the Stoic Ranger dope cards: point mass, G1 or G7 drag tables, air density from your altitude and temperature. Drop is below the line of sight; dial or hold the MIL value up. Lead is for a target crossing at the speed you set. Tap "Field cards → DOPE card" to see this table as a printable card.

1 MIL = 10 cm at 100 m · 1 MOA = 2.91 cm at 100 m · 1 MIL = 3.44 MOA. Also use it to turn a group size into MOA or MIL.

Dial toward where you want the bullets to go. Check against the markings on your own turrets.

Mil relation: distance (m) = size (m) × 1000 ÷ mils. Half a mil misread at 4 mils is a 12 % range error, so measure carefully.

Rifleman's rule: shoot for distance × cos(angle), uphill or downhill alike. Below 10° the difference is usually smaller than your hold.

Navigate

Your position as a grid reference, bearings to a point, and the light for any day. Your position is worked out on this device and never leaves it.

Bearing to a point

From your position above to the target.

Bearings in degrees and NATO mils (6400). Magnetic = true − declination. Take the declination from your map margin or a current declination calculator; it changes with place and year. Grid north differs from true north by the convergence shown.

Saved points

Kept on this device only.

Next 7 days

Times in your device's time zone.

First light = civil dawn (sun 6° below the horizon): enough light to work without a torch. Last light = civil dusk. Nautical twilight (12°) is when the horizon disappears. Computed on the device, accurate to about a minute for the sun and a few minutes for the moon.

Field cards

Business-card size, 85 × 55 mm: one topic per card, front and back. Laminate them, punch a corner, carry the set on a ring or in a wallet. Pick a topic to see both sides.

Prints on A4: fronts on one page, backs on the next. In the print screen choose Save as PDF to keep a file, or print double-sided (flip on long edge) at 100 % scale, then cut along the lines.

Printables

Sheets to print and fill in by hand at the range. Print opens the phone's print screen, where you can print or save a PDF. Share sends the PDF to another app, for example to a print shop.
